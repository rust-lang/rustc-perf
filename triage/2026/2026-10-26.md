pre-merge run, it looks like [#163668](https://github.com/rust-lang/rust/pull/163668) (Remap syntax contexts and expansion ids for det. metadata encoding).

The improvement was not intentional, I asked on the PR and got a reply from @aerooneqq (author):

"We reduced the amount of work in enqueue_syntax_context_for_encoding (ex get_syntax_ctxt_encoding_index) 
which was called for every syntax context and instead of it we implemented the remapping approach, which should be cheaper.

That's just a theory, I didn't properly check it."
